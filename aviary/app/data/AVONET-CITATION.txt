avonet_diet.csv.gz
==================

A compact subset of the AVONET database, bundled with the Aviary add-on to show each
species' diet, trophic level, foraging lifestyle and primary habitat without any network
access.

Contents
--------
10,661 bird species keyed on eBird-taxonomy scientific names (the taxonomy BirdNET-Go
emits), with five columns retained verbatim from AVONET's `AVONET2_eBird` sheet:

    species    <- Species2
    niche      <- Trophic.Niche
    level      <- Trophic.Level
    lifestyle  <- Primary.Lifestyle
    habitat    <- Habitat

Regenerate with `python scripts/build_diet_table.py` (see that script for details). No
values are altered; the mapping from AVONET's terms to the plain-English food
descriptions shown in the UI lives in `app/traits.py`.

Source and citation
-------------------
Tobias, J. A., Sheard, C., Pigot, A. L., et al. (2022). AVONET: morphological, ecological
and geographical data for all birds. Ecology Letters, 25(3), 581-597.
https://doi.org/10.1111/ele.13898

Dataset: Tobias, Joseph (2021). AVONET: morphological, ecological and geographical data
for all birds. figshare. https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.16586228
